# Why Physical Notebooks & Doodles Still Beat Digital Note Apps in 2026

Every year there is a new "second brain" app with AI graph databases, bidirectional links, and markdown shortcuts.

Yet whenever I need to solve a complex architectural dilemma—like state synchronization in WebGL or managing multi-agent context limits—I always close the laptop, pull out an authentic grid notebook, and grab a pencil.

---

## Why?

- **Zero friction:** No keyboard shortcuts, no app loading screens, no sync conflicts.
- **Spatial freedom:** You can draw an arrow that loops around a paragraph, cross out an idea with three furious strokes, and sketch a crooked state machine in 5 seconds.
- **Physical memory:** Your brain remembers the physical corner of the page where you scribbled that idea.

That's why I designed this portfolio as a lined notebook with a wooden pencil cursor and authentic paper texture. Sometimes the oldest tools are still the sharpest.
